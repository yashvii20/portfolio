IEEE Xplore Scopus Indexed ICACRS 2024 Published Conference Paper

Real-Time Sitting Student Posture Detection for Students in Classroom Environment

Yashvi Patel, et al. • Presented at ICACRS 2024 (International Conference on Automation, Computing and Renewable Systems)
Abstract: Prolonged improper sitting posture during classroom instruction leads to severe musculoskeletal strain, chronic back pain, and reduced cognitive engagement among students. This research proposes a real-time, non-invasive posture monitoring and correction framework powered by Python and Google MediaPipe Pose estimation. By analyzing spatial angular deviations in the spinal vector and craniovertebral angle across 33 keypoints, the system detects slouching, asymmetric weight distribution, and forward head posture, triggering automated ergonomic feedback alerts with minimal computational overhead.
  • ✓ Real-time 33-landmark 3D pose extraction via MediaPipe running at 30+ FPS
  • ✓ Custom angular thresholding algorithms for spinal vector & craniovertebral angles
  • ✓ Automated visual and acoustic feedback loop for real-time posture correction
  • ✓ Peer-reviewed, presented at ICACRS 2024, and permanently indexed on Scopus
